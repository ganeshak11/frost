# Frost changes, 19 November 2026

Everything from this round of work, in order. All of it is committed and pushed to `main`.

## 1. Quick Controls, rebuilt (`ac3a231`)

- The old widget (`widgets/org.frost.controls`) was deleted and rebuilt as an iPhone-style
  control centre on a 4×4 grid of glass tiles:
  - Round Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Airplane and Tailscale buttons that turn red when on. Right-click
    Wi-Fi or Bluetooth for its list, or click around the buttons for a details page.
  - Short brightness and volume sliders, one tile wide.
  - Focus (Do Not Disturb), Night Light and mic mute.
  - Sound (outputs, inputs, per-app volume), region screenshot, System Settings and Power.
- **Bug fixed:** scrolling the Wi-Fi list no longer changes volume or brightness. The list
  pages now catch the mouse wheel themselves.
- The card is now 288×288, and `desktop.sh` matches.
- The same commit included work already in the working tree: Frost Notes moving to **Alt+Y**,
  and a fix for Spotlight's results drawing over each other.

## 2. Smoother sliders and the island width bug (`bfc44a3`)

- **Sliders:**
  - The fill follows the pointer every frame; the system is told at most every 40 ms, and
    once more with the exact value on release.
  - Grabbing a slider moves it from where it is instead of jumping, and a click goes straight
    to that spot.
  - Touchpad scrolling is proportional; a mouse wheel moves 5% a notch.
  - Moving the brightness slider no longer pops the on-screen indicator (shared
    `widgets/shared/Levels.qml`, so the drop-down Control Centre benefits too).
- **Island bug:** it sometimes grew only downward. The black shape was still tied to the small
  pill's width, so when the pill changed (charging, mic, camera) while the island was open,
  its width got pulled back. Fixed by giving the shape plain starting values. The island also
  checks its own size four times a second while open and logs a
  `Dynamic Island: size guard` line if it ever has to correct it.
- Test hook: `plasmawindowed org.frost.island frost-test:open`.

## 3. Connected Ecosystem, restyled (`8bd5527`)

- Three glass modules in the Quick Controls style (`widgets/org.frost.devices`):
  - **This laptop:** Wi-Fi or Ethernet, network name, local IP.
  - **Phone:** KDE Connect status, a battery capsule, and Send and Ring buttons.
  - **Tailscale:** your address and up to three devices, online first.
- Click any address to copy it.

## 4. Four new features (`2b97d61`)

- **Charging pill:** plugging in widens the island into a short "Charging" bar whose green
  battery fills up to the current percentage.
- **Screenshot thumbnail:** appears on the island after a screenshot. Drag it into any app,
  click it to open, or use Copy and Show in Folder. It comes from Spectacle's notification,
  the clipboard when Spectacle only copied, or a new file in `~/Pictures/Screenshots`.
- **Drop to phone:** drag files onto the phone module in Connected Ecosystem to send them over
  KDE Connect, a bit like AirDrop.
- **Shortcut Sheet** (`widgets/org.frost.shortcuts`): **Meta+/** or `?` in Spotlight shows every
  Frost, window, screenshot and app shortcut as keycaps, read live from
  `~/.config/kglobalshortcutsrc`. A held Meta key can't be detected in Plasma, which is why
  it's on Meta+/. `desktop.sh` adds it beside the island.

## 5. Markdown in Frost Notes (`68c3212`)

- Styled as you type: headings, bold, italic, strike, code and code blocks, lists, checkboxes,
  quotes and links. The file stays plain `.md`.
- Enter continues lists and checkboxes (Enter on an empty item ends the list), Tab and
  Shift+Tab indent, Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I / Ctrl+K format the selection, Ctrl+Enter or a click on
  `[ ]` ticks a checkbox, Ctrl+click opens a link.
- **Ctrl+E** (or the Preview button) switches to a rendered preview.

## Where things are stored

- **Notes:** plain `.md` files in `~/.local/share/frost-notes/`, saved as you type; they survive
  reboots. Deleted notes go to `~/.local/share/frost-notes/trash/`.
- **Screenshots taken from the clipboard:** `/run/user/1000/frost-shots/`, which is in memory
  and wiped at every reboot; captures older than an hour are also deleted when a new one is
  taken. Screenshots Spectacle saves itself stay where Spectacle put them.
